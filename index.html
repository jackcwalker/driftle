How to play

Find the named country on the unlabelled reconstructed world. Click to place your pin, then Pick. Four 75-million-year animations carry you from 300 Ma to today, with up to five guesses. Find the country once and the remaining rounds receive full points automatically. The drift continues all the way to today before the final results appear; you do not need any more picks.

Land anywhere inside the country's reconstructed area for full points: 5,000 / 4,000 / 3,000 / 2,000 / 1,000 across the five eras. Misses are scored by great-circle distance to the nearest country edge, not a hidden point. The final map highlights the modern country in gold.

Numbered pins on continental land travel with their source plate. Ocean clicks or ambiguous plate overlaps have no supported drift assignment; those pins are grey and stay fixed. Each pick shows your distance from the target, points earned, and how you did compared with the last pick.

Trackpad: two-finger scroll to pan, pinch to zoom. Phone: one-finger drag to pan, two-finger pinch to zoom; tap to pick. Mouse: drag to pan, Ctrl+wheel to zoom. Use +/− or ↺ to reset the view. Keyboard: focus map, arrows to move, Enter to place; Tab to Pick. Shift+arrows makes smaller steps.

These are reconstructed modern coastlines, not ancient shorelines. Modern countries did not exist at these ages. The game follows modern territory split across the model's plates, without continental deformation. Only countries with at least 99.99% model-supported area across all rounds enter the daily pool. Tiny unsupported fragments are omitted at older ages; they are not assigned invented positions.
