How to play

Find the named country on the unlabelled reconstructed world. Click to place your pin, then Pick. Four 75-million-year animations carry you from 300 Ma to today, with five guesses in total.

Scoring uses a representative interior point, not the whole country's ancient territory. A gold target marker is shown when you finish. Earlier and closer guesses earn more points: maximum 5,000 / 4,000 / 3,000 / 2,000 / 1,000, reduced with great-circle distance (1,800 km scale).

Numbered pins on continental land travel with their source plate. Ocean clicks or ambiguous plate overlaps have no supported drift assignment; those pins are grey and stay fixed. Each pick shows your distance from the target, points earned, and how you did compared with the last pick.

Trackpad: two-finger scroll to pan, pinch to zoom. Phone: one-finger drag to pan, two-finger pinch to zoom; tap to pick. Mouse: drag to pan, Ctrl+wheel to zoom. Use +/− or ↺ to reset the view. Keyboard: focus map, arrows to move, Enter to place; Tab to Pick. Shift+arrows makes smaller steps.

These are reconstructed modern coastlines, not ancient shorelines. Modern countries did not exist at these ages. Only supported interior points are included.
